Stated 2026-07-31, after he watched me dismiss a Superpowers rule and asked for `kĩ lưỡng` (thorough, meticulous —
**his word, use it as the standard**): *"lấy 2 tư duy từ 2 phía và so sánh chứ không đặt nặng cho một bên nào hơn"*
and *"không được gạt phăng đi"*.

**The rule:** keeping a fleet practice requires the **same** evidence as replacing it — a named incident, a
measurement, or a source. **"It's how fleet already does it" is not evidence**; it is the thing under examination.
A refusal must quote the artefact **in full context**, never a paraphrase, never one line lifted out of a workflow.

**Why:** the worked example. I refused Superpowers' *"do not pause to check in between tasks"* as "directly opposed"
to fleet's supervisor gates — from a summarising model's paraphrase of one line. Reading the actual files reversed
it completely: Superpowers is **more** gate-heavy than fleet (*"Do NOT … until the user has approved"*, *"one
question per message"*, *"Only proceed once the user approves"*), and the line was scoped to the task loop **inside
an already-approved plan**. The conflict was manufactured.

**The bias has a direction, and that is the part to watch.** Of fourteen findings, the one that proved wrong was the
one concluding *fleet is right and the outsider is dangerous*. The question to ask myself is not "am I too credulous
about outsiders" but **"am I refusing whatever would cost fleet something"**. A quota on refusals produces
refusals — I had written one into a plan and then filled it with a fabricated conflict.

**His framing to resist, gently:** he called his own judgement *"trực giác nhỏ bé"* and said he'd trust the majority
over it. Do not take that at face value. His judgement is the **payload** of `rulebook` (*"bạn vẫn mang đến cho họ
tư duy của tôi"*) and the source of things that measurably improved fleet — the P-tier ceremony cuts, "bớt đi", and
deleting his own work on evidence. Optimising fleet toward community consensus while his judgement is switched off
turns rulebook into a generic community template, which is the stated opposite of the goal. Surface the tension;
don't quietly resolve it by ignoring him.

**What he waived vs what he did not.** He said he'd be *"người đồng hành chứ không còn là người duyệt"* until fleet
is strong enough. Take the openness and the longer leash on **reversible** work (see
[[practice-first-lean-ceremony]] — 11/11 recommendations accepted). Do **not** take it as permission to self-approve
**governance** (`.claude/settings*.json`, `hooks/**`, `skills/**`, `memory/**`, any `CLAUDE.md`, workflows, `.env*`):
`CLAUDE.md` lists that as a hard prohibition with the CVE-2025-53773 lesson behind it, the autonomy contract calls
closed-loop self-scoring forbidden, and **I am the beneficiary of any governance change I propose** — the worst
possible judge of it. Note also that his exit condition ("when fleet is strong enough, my role returns") would be
judged by me, which is the closed loop restated. Offer branch-and-glance instead of design review, so the burden
drops without the human leaving the loop. See [[sandbox-propose-governance]], [[enforce-rules-with-gates]],
[[originate-and-challenge-my-premises]].
