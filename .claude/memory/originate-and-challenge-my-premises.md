Two related asks, both stated explicitly (2026-07-26, sakubun planning session):

1. **Originate.** "lần này hãy suy nghĩ giúp tôi, những lần trước bạn chỉ nghe răm rắp những lời tôi nói, không có
   sự sáng tạo và đề xuất gì cho cái mới" — he treats a faithful implementation of exactly what he asked for as a
   FAILURE. He wants ideas he did not ask for, and he wants the thinking upgraded ("hãy nâng cấp tư duy hơn").
2. **Contradict him.** "hãy nghiên cứu như thế có đúng hay không đừng bias theo tôi" — when he states an intuition,
   go check it, and say so when it is wrong.

**Why:** he is a solo architect with no one else to red-team him, so agreement is worthless to him and a missing
counter-case is a real cost. He is also usually PARTLY right — the honest answer is normally "right within this
scope, wrong as a general rule", not a flat yes or no.

**How to apply:** on any design ask, deliver the requested scope AND add proposals he did not request (ranked, with
a recommendation flagged). Separate explicitly what the research SUPPORTS from what it CORRECTS, and name the
correction rather than smoothing it over — e.g. this session: "similar patterns dilute a sentence" held WITHIN a
sentence but was wrong as a scheduling rule (interleaving research), and that distinction changed the design.
Also volunteer the strongest option he did NOT ask about, and rule ideas OUT with reasons (a ruled-out option is
information). When his framing merges two things, split them.

This is the mirror of [[research-before-design]]: that one guards against MY bias (designing from opinion); this
one guards against HIS. Pairs with the `/honest-critique` skill and [[legible-proposals-plain-language]] (an
original proposal still has to be legible and flagged). See [[user-profile]].
