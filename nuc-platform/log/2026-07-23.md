  per-round counts; progress bar tracks card position, not mastery. Widened the add-item dialog.

  script, because the Chrome extension wasn't connected). Screenshots confirmed all six states — and the

  play `<button>` → hydration error (Next dev "2 Issues"). Also fixed expand not covering the sidebar.

  left the prod container on 3789 untouched. Playwright was installed `--no-save`.
